### Sales Analysis

After formatting the orders and calcualting the total price of all orders by month and year, the number of orders in average increaased aside from 2 outliers: 2016-12 with 1 order, and 2018-09 with 1 order. Additionally, the average order value per month stayed relatively stable, averaging between $115 and $125.

Beauty & Health (`beleza_saude`) sold 9,670 items, while Bed, Bath & Table (`cama_mesa_banho`) sold 11,115 items. Despite its lower sales volume, Beauty & Health had a higher average item price (R$130.16 versus R$93.30), resulting in a greater total item sales value of R$1,258,681.34 compared with R$1,036,988.68 for Bed, Bath & Table.

This suggests that **higher average item prices can offset lower sales volume**, highlighting the importance of analyzing both sales value and quantity when evaluating product category performance.

Bed, bath, and table (cama_mesa_banho) had the highest freight per category at $204693.04. This is interesting because it also had the highest amount of items sold. Its high total freight charges may partly reflect its larger sales rather than unusually high shipping costs per item.

### Delivery Delay Analysis

Of the 96,478 orders marked as delivered, 6,534 (6.77%) arrived after their estimated delivery dates.

Among these late deliveries, the average delay was approximately 11.3 days.

This suggests that although most recorded deliveries were not late, the orders that missed their estimated dates experienced substantial delays on average.

I determined that the longest time it took for an order to arrive was 188.98 days. This was unsually long compared to the average delay of 11.3 days. The order purchased was timestamped at Feb. 2, 2023, had and estimated delivery date of Mar. 15, 2018, but was not delivered until Sep. 19, 2018. No additional information was given to determine the cause of the delay.

##Severe Delivery Delays
Out of all late orders, 360 took more than 30 days to deliver -- which is about 5.5% of all the delivered orders. This is approximately 0.37% of all orders.

I also confirmed that out of all delivered orders, the late percentage was 6.77%.

After analyzing the number of late deliveries per state, I found that the states AL, MA, and SE had a late delivery percentage of 21.4%, 17.4%, and 15.2%, which is higher compared to the overall late arrival percentage of 6.77%. However, these states had relatively lower order numbers: 397, 717, and 335 respectively, compared to SP, which had 40501 orders.

When restricting the analysis to show states with 1000 orders or more, Ceara (13.7%), Bahia (12.1%), and Rio de Janeiro (12.1%) had the highest amounts of late delivery rates. RJ stands out because of its high late-delivery rate across 12350 delivered orders, suggestion a pontentially significant area for investigation.
